Balanced Minecraft Hardcore · item redesign proposal

Death is a journey. The items should know it.

The Totem of Eternity, the three Eternal tools, the Eternal Bundle and the Navigator's Amulet, redrawn and rethought: what they look like, how you get them, what they do. Gameplay came first, then an adversarial review, then the pixels. Nothing here is code yet.

16x16 textures drawn at the game's own scale, shown 3x. The totem and the full bundle play their .mcmeta animations; the amulet's needle spins the way it does in the Nether.

Four rules behind every choice

Death is a journey

On Normal you wake 23,333 blocks from your bed. Every item serves the walk home, or the new start where you landed.

Eternity comes from what cheats death

A Totem of Undying refuses death. Echo Shards are what remains: the recovery compass is made of them. Crying Obsidian brings you back: it is the respawn anchor's stone. The real gates are an evoker (only they drop the totem) and an Ancient City (the only source of shards). The tools add the Wither.

Eternity has weight

Each Eternal Item you carry pushes your respawn about 6,100 blocks farther. Nobody sees it today. Shown, it turns carrying eternal items into a decision.

Mortal and eternal read at a glance

Eternal: netherite black, gold, echo cyan, a small gold ring. Mortal: warm copper and a night-blue face. The amulet is the only mortal one, and it looks it.

The mechanic everything hangs on

The weight of eternity

Your respawn is a random point on a circle around your bed. The circle grows with the difficulty and with every Eternal Item in your inventory, between 5,000 and 60,000 blocks with the default config.

Progression

The path

1 · First hours

Navigator's Amulet

Iron, redstone, copper, amethyst, string. Before the Nether. Today it needs 4 Eyes of Ender, so it arrives after blazes and endermen, while you have no coordinates at all.

2 · Mid to late game

Totem, then Bundle

A raid or a woodland mansion for the Totem of Undying, an Ancient City for the Echo Shards, crying obsidian from ruined portals or bartering. The Eternal Bundle is the first eternity you own.

3 · Endgame

One Totem of Eternity and one Nether Star per tool, on top of your own netherite tool, at the smithing table. Three tools, three Withers.

  • Smithing table: the Totem of Eternity in the template slot, your netherite tool, a Nether Star. The tool keeps its enchantments, its name and its wear. Today's crafting-table recipe eats them.
  • No dragon egg. There is one per world, so today only one player per world can ever own an Eternal Pickaxe.

Sword

Echo Strike

A full-strength hit echoes to the nearest other enemy within 5 blocks, for half the damage: a cyan arc between them. It jumps because an echo on the same target would land inside its invulnerability frames and add nothing.

Axe

Echo Felling

Cut a log and the blow runs up the trunk: the whole tree falls. Only trees with natural leaves, capped at 64 logs, so it never fells your house. Sneak to cut a single log.

Pickaxe

Echo Sense

Mine an ore and the stone answers: the same ore within 8 blocks glows through walls for 5 seconds. Done with glowing block displays spawned by the server, so no client rendering code.

Mortal · first hours

Navigator's Amulet

It points home and it reads the stars. That is all, and that is enough.

X 1,284 · Y 72 · Z -3,907 · 23,407 · If you fall now: 29,444

Mockup: night, open sky, the amulet in the offhand. The needle in the item points home; the star fix (use) writes your coordinates, the distance home and your exile radius to the action bar.

The needle

Drawn in the item itself, like a vanilla compass, and it points to your Home. You choose Home on purpose: sneak and use the amulet on a bed or a lodestone. It is stored on you, not on the amulet, and a camp bed you sleep in does not move it. Overworld only: in the Nether and the End it spins.

The star fix

Use it under open sky: exact X, Y, Z, the distance home, and the radius you would wake on if you died now. Under a roof, underground, in the Nether or the End, the stars are hidden. Readings go to the action bar, so they work on servers.

Starlight

Three fixes, and they refill each night you spend under open sky. No item to feed it, nothing to break. Today it has 10 uses, then you craft another with 4 more Eyes of Ender.

Eight of the needle's headings. The game would use 32, picked by a compass-style "angle" property, as the vanilla compass does.

Not one-shot

What the review changed

A first version went to a clean-context reviewer told to break it, with the mod's code and the vanilla sources to check against. Every claim below was re-checked before it changed the design.

AmuletA screen-wide compass ribbon with Home, Last death and Mark pins, all with distances, gave away the whole "being lost" tension and duplicated the recovery compass. The needle moved into the item, points home only, Overworld only.
HomeClicking any bed sets your spawn, even by day, so a camp bed would have stolen your home. Home is now set on purpose.
ChargesAmethyst charges became Starlight that refills at night: same pacing, no inventory chore.
BundleFour stacks would have replaced the Eternal tools and opened a smuggling hole through nested bundles. Capacity stays vanilla.
SwordAn echo on the same target lands in its invulnerability frames: at full swing speed it adds no damage at all. The echo now jumps to another enemy.
Statsv1 lowered the tools to netherite plus a notch. That betrayed "very powerful". They stay; only the axe is in question.
AxeFelling every connected log would fell a log cabin. Natural trees only, capped.
RepairRepairing with Echo Shards spent the scarcest ingredient. Gold blocks instead.
Found on the way · bugs in the released mod

Worth fixing before any redesign

Eternal tools cannot be enchanted on 1.21.1

High

Since 1.21, enchantments decide what they apply to through item tags (#minecraft:swords and friends), and the mod ships no tag files. So no book and no enchanting table works on them, on all three loaders. Read from the code, not yet tested in game. The fix is data only: add the three tools to the vanilla tags.

The Eternal Bundle's filled texture never shows

Medium

The model switches on the "filled" property, but vanilla registers that property for its own bundle only and the mod registers none. Every version, every loader: the bundle always looks empty.

On a dedicated server the amulet burns a use and shows nothing

Medium

The coordinates go through on-screen text that only exists on a physical client, but the durability is spent on the server. The changelog says the text only shows in singleplayer; it does not say the use is lost.

A respawn anchor exiles you inside the Nether

Check

The exile is centred on your spawn point in its own dimension, so with an anchor you wake 23,333 Nether blocks away: 186,664 in Overworld terms. Read from the code; it may be intended.

Buckets, not estimates

What it costs to build

Data only

JSON and PNG: the new textures and animations (including the 32 needle headings), the crafting and smithing recipes, and the item tags that fix enchanting on 1.21.1. Same files on every branch; 1.21.1's recipe format differs.

Small code

One client registration per loader for the needle and the bundle's looks. Home on the player, written into the amulet for the needle. The star fix: sky check, action bar, night refill. Tooltips, gold repair.

New systems

The three echo abilities: Echo Strike's jump, Echo Felling's natural-tree check, Echo Sense's glowing block displays. Each wants a real test in runClient.

Everything ships to seven branches, and 1.21.1 is ported by hand. The data-only bucket alone already delivers the new look, the new recipes and the enchanting fix.

Your calls

  1. The axe. Keep 18 damage (21 with Sharpness V, so it one-shots any 20-health mob: zombies, skeletons, creepers), or trim it to 15?
  2. Starlight. Three fixes that refill each night, or free fixes under the sky with only a cooldown?
  3. Echo Sense. The most expensive ability for the most convenience-like effect. Keep it, or give the pickaxe something else?
  4. The 50-minute blasting recipe that turns a Totem of Eternity into the same totem. What is it for?
